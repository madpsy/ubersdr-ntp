Still settling

 

  • Clock rate not yet measured. Until it is, nothing takes out the receiver's sample-clock drift — around 24 ppm, or 1.4 ms a minute — so the upstream NTP offset climbs steadily and the difference between the classes goes with it.
  • NTP filter keeps eight polls; at a 64 s poll that is about 8 minutes just to fill once.
  • Sample-clock fit regresses over a 5-minute window, per source.
  • Sources confirm a first decoded time against their own history before counting, and one that recovers waits 180 s before it is taken back, so the consensus can change shape for a while.
